Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don’t

Built for creators, professionals, and anyone who wants their links organised in one place.

🔗

Unlimited Links

Add as many links as you want — social profiles, websites, music, anything. Organise them with custom sections and dividers, and toggle each link on or off without deleting it.

🕵️

Stealth Redirect Links

Create shareable redirect links (/r/...) that work silently in the background. They never appear on your public profile page, but every click is fully tracked in your analytics — perfect for email campaigns, paid ads, or private sharing.

✨ Hidden from public page
📊

Per-Link Analytics

Drill into any individual link for its own dedicated analytics page. See total clicks, unique clickers, click-through rate, daily trend chart, device breakdown, top countries, top cities, referrers, and a paginated click log — all scoped to that single link.

✨ New in v0.1.1
📈

Real-Time Analytics

See every click and profile view as it happens. Know which links perform best, track your CTR, and monitor how your audience grows — all from a single dashboard with selectable date ranges (7, 30, 90, 365 days).

🎨

Full Customisation

Custom backgrounds (solid, gradient, or image), island card styles (frosted glass, solid, gradient), button shapes and fills, font family and size, name and bio colours — make your page look exactly like you.

🌍

Country & City Insights

Geo analytics built right in. Track which countries and cities your visitors come from, with automatic fallback via Cloudflare and Vercel proxy headers — no extra config needed in production.

📱

Device Breakdown

Know whether your audience is on desktop, mobile, or tablet. Device split is tracked across both profile views and individual link clicks, giving you a complete picture of how people engage.

⚡

Lightning Fast & Self-Hosted

Your page loads instantly with no bloat. Because Link Branch is fully self-hosted on FastAPI + SQLite, you own your data entirely — no vendor lock-in, no surprise pricing.

🔐

Secure by Default

bcrypt password hashing, password-policy enforcement, session-based auth with configurable SameSite and HTTPS-only flags, CORS origin whitelist, rate limiting on all API endpoints, and a frontend error logger.
